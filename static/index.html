Keep a copy of your progress
Your records live in the app’s data folder. Export a JSON backup regularly, especially before moving or replacing the app folder.

What this app does—and doesn’t do
Scores use text matching and a cybersecurity / IT skills dictionary. They cannot verify proficiency, eligibility, vacancy status or interview chances. Paste jobs from any site using “Add a job”; blocked sites are not scraped. Applications are submitted on the employer’s website, never automatically.
Live search needs internet access. Your saved jobs, notes, basic matching and basic tailoring work offline while this local app is running. Optional AI must already be installed.

Inside the match score
Scores run from 1 to 100 after you save a CV and a full JD. They measure textual alignment, not hiring likelihood.
Skills & tools60
Role-title vocabulary15
Broader JD vocabulary10
Experience years10
Certifications5
Components without usable evidence are left out; remaining weights are normalized to 100. Years use your entered total experience, not verified domain-specific tenure. “Preferred” skills count less than other skills when clearly labeled.
Alias matching recognizes names such as SCCM / MECM and Entra ID / Azure AD. The dictionary is strongest for cybersecurity and IT roles. Missing keywords are not proof you lack a skill. Visa, language, salary and location eligibility must be checked separately.
